Grading engine

Built for footage that starts flat

Every tool in LogLab exists to take your log footage somewhere better — accurately, and in real time.

Accurate log transforms

Purpose-built log-to-Rec.709 transforms for Sony S-Log, Canon C-Log, DJI D-Log and Apple Log — tuned per format, not a generic curve.

Real-time Metal pipeline

Every adjustment renders live on-device through a custom Metal pipeline — full-resolution preview, no waiting for proxies.

Hands-on manual controls

Log format, exposure, saturation, contrast and grain — direct, precise sliders for grading exactly the way you want.

Preloaded LUT library

A curated set of ready-to-use LUTs for people who'd rather grade with one tap — including a set of cinematic, movie-inspired looks.

Import your own LUTs

Bring in DaVinci Resolve-format LUTs and grade with your own studio looks, right on your phone.

Pro-grade export

Export in ProRes 422 HQ or ProRes 4444 for a finishing pipeline that keeps every bit of the grade intact.

Interface

Every format, every aspect ratio

LogLab adapts to whatever you shoot — square Clips grid for browsing, full manual control when you need it.
