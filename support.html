Support

We're happy to help

Running into a bug, have a feature request, or need help with your LogLab Pro purchase? Reach out and we'll get back to you as soon as we can.

Contact

Email matgarciadesign@gmail.com and include your device model and iOS version if you're reporting a bug. It helps us track things down faster.

Restoring LogLab Pro

LogLab Pro is a one-time purchase tied to your Apple Account. It's unlocked for life, with no subscription to manage. If Pro isn't showing as unlocked on a device, open LogLab and tap "Restore Purchases" while signed into the Apple ID you bought it with. See our Refund Policy for how refund requests work.
