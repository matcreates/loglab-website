Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 9, 2026

This policy explains what information LogLab ("LogLab", "we", "us") collects, how it's used, and the choices you have. LogLab is developed by Mathis Garcia. If you have questions, contact matgarciadesign@gmail.com.

Information we collect

Account information. Creating a LogLab account requires an email address and password. Authentication is handled by Firebase Authentication, a service operated by Google — your password is managed directly by Firebase and is never visible to or stored by us in plain text.

Your video content. Clips, grades, and LUTs you work with in LogLab are stored locally on your device only. We do not upload, transmit, or store your video content on any server, and we cannot access it.

Purchase information. LogLab Pro subscriptions are sold and billed entirely through Apple's App Store using StoreKit. We do not receive or store your payment card details. We may receive a non-identifying signal from Apple indicating whether a subscription is active, in order to unlock Pro features.

We do not use third-party analytics or advertising SDKs in LogLab.

How we use information

  • To create and manage your account, and to authenticate sign-in
  • To provide account features such as password reset
  • To determine whether LogLab Pro features should be unlocked for your account
  • To respond to support requests you send us

Third-party services

LogLab relies on the following third-party services to operate:

  • Firebase Authentication (Google) — account creation and sign-in. See Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Apple App Store / StoreKit — processes all purchases and subscriptions. See Apple's Privacy Policy.

Data sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We do not share your data with third parties other than the service providers listed above, and only as necessary for them to provide their service to us.

Data retention and deletion

We retain your account information for as long as your account is active. To request deletion of your account and associated data, email matgarciadesign@gmail.com and we'll process your request promptly.

Children's privacy

LogLab is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, or delete your personal information, or to object to or restrict certain processing. To exercise any of these rights, contact us at matgarciadesign@gmail.com.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date above.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to matgarciadesign@gmail.com.
