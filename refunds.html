Refund Policy

Last updated: September 9, 2026

LogLab is distributed exclusively through Apple's App Store, and all purchases — including LogLab Pro subscriptions — are billed and processed entirely by Apple. This means Apple handles all payments and refunds, and LogLab does not have access to your payment information or the ability to issue refunds directly.

Requesting a refund

To request a refund for a LogLab purchase or subscription, use Apple's official refund process:

  • Visit reportaproblem.apple.com and sign in with the Apple ID used for the purchase
  • Or on your device, go to Settings > [your name] > Subscriptions to manage or cancel a subscription

Apple's refund decisions are made according to their own policies. Learn more at Apple's official refund support page.

Cancelling a subscription

Canceling LogLab Pro stops future billing but does not automatically refund the current billing period — you'll keep Pro access until the end of the period you already paid for. Cancel any time from Settings > [your name] > Subscriptions on your device.

Something wrong with the app?

If you're requesting a refund because of a bug, missing feature, or something not working as expected, we'd genuinely like to help — even though we can't process the refund ourselves. Email us at matgarciadesign@gmail.com and we'll do what we can to fix it.
